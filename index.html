Ringkasan Rumus Asam-Basa

1. Asam Kuat

[H⁺] = a × M

pH = -log[H⁺]

2. Basa Kuat

[OH⁻] = b × M

pOH = -log[OH⁻]  |  pH = 14 - pOH

3. Basa Lemah

[OH⁻] = √(Kb × M)

pOH = -log[OH⁻]

4. Mol & Massa

n = M × V (dalam Liter)

Massa = n × Mr
